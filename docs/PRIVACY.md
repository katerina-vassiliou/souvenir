# Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Souvenir keeps everything on your device.

When you choose Keep in collection, it saves the image, a small thumbnail, the page title and address, the element's name and size, and the time. Your settings are saved too. All of this sits in your browser's local extension storage and goes nowhere else.

Souvenir has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It never sends your souvenirs, the pages you visit or your settings to me or to anyone else.

It doesn't have standing access to your sites. Chrome gives it access to the current tab only when you start a capture from the right-click menu or the shortcut, and it reads that page only then. The README lists the permissions and why each is there.

The images and fonts on the page you're collecting from are loaded by the page itself, from the same places it already loads them from. Souvenir makes no other network requests.

You can delete any souvenir, or clear the whole collection, from the collection. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
