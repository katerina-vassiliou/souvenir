Collect anything from a page

Turn any container into an image on your clipboard, then keep the best ones here.

  1. Start. Right-click an element and choose Collect element, or press ⌘⇧E with the pointer over it.
  2. Choose. Move the pointer to highlight a container. Press ↑ for the parent or ↓ to go back.
  3. Collect. Click or press Enter. It’s copied, ready to paste. Esc cancels.
  4. Keep. Choose Keep in collection on the message that appears.

In your collection
